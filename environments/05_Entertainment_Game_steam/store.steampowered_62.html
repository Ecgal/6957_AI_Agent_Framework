About This Game

Bethesda Game Studios, the award-winning creators of Fallout 3 and The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim, welcome you to the world of Fallout 4 their most ambitious game ever, and the next generation of open-world gaming. As the sole survivor of Vault 111, you enter a world destroyed by nuclear war. Every second is a fight for survival, and every choice is yours. Only you can rebuild and determine the fate of the Wasteland. Welcome home.

Key Features:

  • Freedom and Liberty! Do whatever you want in a massive open world with hundreds of locations, characters, and quests. Join multiple factions vying for power or go it alone, the choices are all yours.
  • Youre S.P.E.C.I.A.L! Be whoever you want with the S.P.E.C.I.A.L. character system. From a Power Armored soldier to the charismatic smooth talker, you can choose from hundreds of Perks and develop your own playstyle.
  • Super Deluxe Pixels! An all-new next generation graphics and lighting engine brings to life the world of Fallout like never before. From the blasted forests of the Commonwealth to the ruins of Boston, every location is packed with dynamic detail.
  • Violence and V.A.T.S.! Intense first or third person combat can also be slowed down with the new dynamic Vault-Tec Assisted Targeting System (V.A.T.S) that lets you choose your attacks and enjoy cinematic carnage.
  • Collect and Build! Collect, upgrade, and build thousands of items in the most advanced crafting system ever. Weapons, armor, chemicals, and food are just the beginning - you can even build and manage entire settlements.

A brief overview of Fallout 4
- Maybe. - Sarcasm. - No. - Yes.
+
Graphics acceptable so far. Atmosphere. Shooting. Lots of enemies. Large elaborate world. Ability to equip settlements. A huge variety of additional quests. A huge number of weapons that can be modified in different ways. The ability to install mods in the game menu (Unfortunately, achievements are disabled in this case). The game is addictive for a long time. Various satellites. Radio stations and soundtrack.
-
The most boring and typical plot. Dialogue system of the same words. You can not teleport from closed locations.
Result
Fallout 4 - The best part in the series for me. However, unlike New Vegas, the game is very much inferior in the dialogue system and plot. But it excels in new features. I highly recommend playing this game.

Fallout 4 is a game that falls short of expectations, failing to live up to the high standards set by its predecessors in the Fallout franchise. While the game has its moments of fun and excitement, there are a variety of issues that make it difficult to fully enjoy. One of the biggest problems with Fallout 4 is the lack of depth in its storytelling. The game's plot is predictable and lacks the complexity and nuance that fans of the series have come to expect. The game's dialogue options are also limited, with little variation in the responses players can choose, making it difficult to roleplay or feel truly invested in the game's world and characters. Another issue with Fallout 4 is the lack of meaningful choices and consequences. The game's choices often feel inconsequential, with little impact on the game's overall story or world. This makes the game feel more like a series of fetch quests and side missions than a true open-world RPG. The game's graphics and technical performance are also problematic. The game suffers from frequent glitches and bugs, with textures popping in and out of view and NPCs getting stuck in scenery. The game's graphics are also underwhelming, with dull and uninspired environments that lack the detail and polish of other games in the genre. Finally, the game's combat system is clunky and unresponsive, with a lack of variety in weapons and abilities that makes combat feel repetitive and tedious. Overall, Fallout 4 is a game that fails to deliver on the promise of its predecessors. Its lackluster storytelling, limited dialogue options, inconsequential choices, technical issues, and uninspired combat make it a difficult game to fully enjoy. While there are moments of fun and excitement to be had, they are overshadowed by the game's numerous flaws.
